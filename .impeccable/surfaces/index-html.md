# Surface: index.html (Persuade, mobile first)

Audience: young family in Trelew, arrives from WhatsApp/social on a phone. Action: WhatsApp inquiry. Proof: 12 real photos and listing facts only. Constraints: Propietas brand wins (Montserrat, azul/celeste, logo rules); no unconfirmed claims; static HTML/CSS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the street's own address plate. Argentine enamel street sign, blue field, white letters, rolled border, rivets. It refuses the portal listing template (gallery left, price box right, form).

OWN-WORLD: Propietas azul #2e3092 enamel plates with a white inner keyline and rolled edge, rivets in the corners, Montserrat Black caps for plates, Light for body; celeste #27aae1 only on large text or on azul; whites and greys tinted toward the azul; photos sit on white with thin azul frames. Recognizable with content removed: a column of riveted blue plates on a pale blue-white wall.

STORY: This is a real, lived-in duplex at a known address with a clear price. The visitor understands size, rooms, patio, car entrance, and where it is, believes it because the photos are plain and honest, and taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 wide. Small Propietas logo (color on white strip) at top. Then a large riveted plate "LOVE PARRY 159" with "TRELEW" beneath, filling the width. Under it the cover photo and the price "USD 70.000" on a smaller plate. WhatsApp button, full width, sits in the first screen. Desktop: plate spans left, cover photo right, same content, button under the plate.

FORM: Chapa de calle, my own top candidate (pick card), seed key 1ea8dc0e; challengers declined.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
