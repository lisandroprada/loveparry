# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS (chosen by the user). Deploy target undecided; publish only with user confirmation.

## Users
Young families in Trelew and the surrounding Chubut region looking to buy their first or next home. They arrive from WhatsApp shares and social media, mostly on a phone, and want to decide quickly whether the place fits and how to ask about it. Investors are a secondary audience, not the lead message.

## Product Purpose
A single-property landing page for Propietas Inmobiliaria that sells one duplex at Love Parry 159, Trelew. Success is a qualified inquiry: a visitor taps the WhatsApp button (wa.me/5492804503151) or leaves a message, already knowing the price, size and location.

## Positioning
A local, family-run agency with matriculated broker backing the operation (M.P. Nº 117 COCICH), selling a ready-to-live-in duplex with real photos and a clear price. Nothing here is a promise beyond the listing facts.

## Operating Context
Inquiries go to the firm's WhatsApp; the office is in Rawson (Gregorio Mayo 106). The property data comes from the firm's own listing on ipropietas.com.ar, so the landing must match it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Property: Love Parry 159, Trelew, Chubut, a few meters from Av. Hipólito Yrigoyen.
- USD 70.000 · 85 m² total · 4 ambientes · 2 dormitorios · 2 baños · 25 years old.
- Patio with fogón; own car entrance.
- Status: for sale, available.
- Undecided / not to be claimed: financing, permuta, deed (escritura) status, expensas, orientation, condition details, exact map pin. Everything else goes through WhatsApp ("consultar").
- Language: Spanish, rioplatense voseo.

## Brand Commitments
Propietas identity only: azul #2e3092, celeste #27aae1, Montserrat, logo rules from `../_shared/brand-manual.md`. Tone is close, human and concrete, with no inflated superlatives. Logos live in `assets/logos/`. Contact data and legal footer come from `../_shared/inmobiliaria.md`.

## Evidence on Hand
12 real photos of the property in `assets/img/foto-01.jpg` to `foto-12.jpg` (cover is portrait 1042×1280). No testimonials, no plans, no renders, no video, no map. Do not fabricate social proof.

## Product Principles
- Lead with the facts a family decides on: price, size, rooms, location.
- One clear action: write on WhatsApp.
- Use only confirmed claims; mark unknowns as "consultá".
- Phone first: fast load, thumb-reachable CTA.
- The firm's matrícula and legal footer are always visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text contrast AA (celeste only for large text or on dark blue), alt text on all photos, respect prefers-reduced-motion.
